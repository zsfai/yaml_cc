Fast triage checklist

  • Convert tabs to spaces; keep one indent width (2 or 4).
  • Ensure every mapping key ends with : plus a space before a same-line value.
  • Quote colons inside plain strings, or use quoted scalars.
  • Re-validate after each fix so line numbers stay meaningful.
