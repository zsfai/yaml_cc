Privacy

Direct answer

YAML.cc tool pages process YAML in your browser and do not upload your pasted files to YAML.cc for validation or conversion. Pages may still request third-party CDN scripts for the editor and parser.

Browser processing

YAML validation, formatting, and conversion on YAML.cc run in your browser using client-side libraries. Your pasted content is not uploaded to YAML.cc servers for those tools.

Third-party scripts

Site CSS is self-hosted. Tool pages may still load CDN scripts for Ace editor, js-yaml, and Ajv (JSON Schema). Those requests go to the respective CDN providers per normal browser behavior.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository linked from the site footer or GitHub presence.
